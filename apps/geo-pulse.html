What this app does

It uses monthly FRED data for industrial production, rates, credit spreads, share prices, real manufacturing/trade sales, unemployment, CPI, sentiment, and global policy uncertainty. It transforms them into the V1 organism state, robust-z normalizes them, then computes the shadow-trajectory λ̄ signal. This is the first pulse test, not a final forecasting model.

Data source: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. If the browser blocks direct FRED access, the app tries a public CORS relay automatically.
