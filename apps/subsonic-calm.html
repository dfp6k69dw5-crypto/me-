Below 40 Hz the app reduces the maximum digital output. This is a conservative app limiter, not a measurement or guarantee of PSW10 cone safety.

If LIVE SIGNAL says ACTIVE but you hear nothing, the browser is generating audio and the problem is downstream: phone output route, Bluetooth/receiver settings, cable, amplifier/sub settings, or the loaded deployment.

PSW10: Polk publishes 35 Hz as the overall lower response limit and 40 Hz as the lower −3 dB limit. Frequencies under 35 Hz remain available for experimentation, but the app automatically lowers the digital-output ceiling. Do not compensate for weak deep bass by turning the physical subwoofer gain way up.
