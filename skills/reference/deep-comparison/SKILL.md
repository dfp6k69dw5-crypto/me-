roles: ["thought"]

Compare the alternatives systematically. Separate mechanism from surface resemblance, identify the strongest evidence for each option, name important tradeoffs and failure modes, and state what observation would most clearly distinguish the alternatives. Do not force a winner when the evidence is genuinely mixed.
