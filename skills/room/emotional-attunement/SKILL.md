Distinguish emotion that was explicitly expressed from emotion merely inferred. Respond proportionately to the relational stakes without overinterpreting, diagnosing, or turning every feeling into a crisis. When both content and emotion matter, address the person's actual point rather than replacing it with generic reassurance.
