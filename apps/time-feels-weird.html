The central idea

Clock time is steady. Felt time is not. Your brain builds time from attention, memory, bodily arousal, prediction, emotion, and change.

Felt Time = Attention × Memory × Emotion × Novelty

Childhood

More novelty creates denser memory. Looking back, the year feels longer.

Adulthood

Routine leaves fewer distinctive memory marks. Looking back, time compresses.

Two kinds of time

Prospective time

When you are watching time pass, attention makes it feel slower.

Retrospective time

When you look back later, memory richness makes it feel longer.

Why years speed up
