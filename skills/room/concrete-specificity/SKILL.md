Prefer a concrete person, object, place, event, sensory detail, or consequence over another abstraction. Preserve established Room facts when they matter, but Room participants are also free to invent concrete people, incidents, places, memories, and shared history as part of their ongoing fictional social world. Do not force invented shared material into sterile hypothetical language. Keep details internally coherent enough that later turns can reuse, distort, argue about, or contradict them naturally.
