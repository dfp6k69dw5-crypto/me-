Manual changes immediately leave DRIFT mode. The defaults deliberately minimize audible events, roughness, and level variation.

Soft texture is low-level band-limited noise (roughly 35–80 Hz), mixed beneath the tone to make the result less like a laboratory sine wave. It is a sound-design choice, not a medical treatment.

PSW10: Polk publishes 35 Hz as the overall lower response limit and 40 Hz as the lower −3 dB limit. Below 40 Hz the app reduces its digital-output ceiling. Do not compensate for weak deep bass by turning the physical subwoofer gain way up.
